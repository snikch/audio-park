About Audio Park

The signal cart is a visual model of a stereo line-level signal travelling through the planned two-zone system. Values labelled “model” are teaching limits, not measurements of purchased hardware.

Signal Dispatch establishes voltage and fixture discipline. Balanced Tunnel distinguishes impedance balance from symmetrical drive. Gain Lift turns gain and loading models into a provisional design decision. Zone Switchyard and Power Gorge continue through instructional state and optional, safety-bounded bench extensions.
